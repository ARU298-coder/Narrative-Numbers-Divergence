Item 1. Business
We design and sell precision components. See Item 1A. Risk Factors for a discussion of risks that may affect us.
Item 1A. Risk Factors
Risks Related to Our Supply Chain
We depend on two suppliers, Northwind Semiconductor Corp. and Delta Precision Ltd., for approximately 41% of our component purchases, and the loss of either supplier will materially and adversely affect our ability to manufacture our products and will significantly reduce our revenue.
Several of our key components are sourced from a single supplier with no qualified alternative. A disruption at Northwind Semiconductor Corp. would require us to redesign products, which will take at least nine months and will materially harm our results of operations.
In fiscal 2023 we entered into a minimum purchase commitment of $64 million with Northwind Semiconductor Corp. that extends through 2026. If our demand falls below the committed volumes we will be required to pay shortfall penalties that will materially reduce our operating income.
Our products are shipped from facilities in Penang, Malaysia and Monterrey, Mexico. Increases in freight costs, port congestion, or delays in customs clearance have increased our delivery times in the past and could continue to do so in the future.
Risks Related to Our Customers
Our three largest customers accounted for 52% of our net revenue in fiscal 2023, and Meridian Automotive Group alone accounted for 27%. The loss of Meridian, or a significant reduction in its orders, will materially and adversely affect our revenue and profitability.
Demand for our products depends on the automotive and industrial end markets, which are cyclical. During the downturn of 2020 our revenue declined 18%, and we expect that a similar downturn will significantly reduce our revenue and cash flow.
Risks Related to Our Indebtedness
Our credit agreement requires us to maintain a total leverage ratio below 3.5 times and contains covenants that restrict our ability to incur additional debt. A breach of these covenants will result in an event of default and will materially impair our liquidity.
We had $120 million of outstanding borrowings under our term loan as of the end of fiscal 2023. Our indebtedness requires us to dedicate a significant portion of our cash flow to debt service, which reduces funds available for capital expenditures.
Risks Related to Competition
We compete with larger companies that have greater financial resources. Competitive pressure may force us to reduce prices, which could reduce our gross margin and may adversely affect our results of operations in future periods.
Legal and Regulatory Risks
We are subject to export control and sanctions laws in the United States and other jurisdictions in which we operate. Violations of these laws could result in fines, loss of export privileges and damage to our reputation with customers and regulators.
Changes in tax law, including changes to the treatment of foreign earnings, could increase our effective tax rate and reduce our net income. We monitor legislative developments in each jurisdiction in which we have operations.
Risks Related to Information Technology
We rely on information technology systems to manage our manufacturing operations. A cybersecurity incident or data breach could disrupt production, expose confidential customer information and result in significant costs and legal liability.

Item 1. Business
We design and sell precision components. See Item 1A. Risk Factors for a discussion of risks that may affect us.
Item 1A. Risk Factors
Risks Related to Our Supply Chain
We depend on certain third parties for a portion of our component purchases, and the loss of one of these suppliers may adversely affect our ability to manufacture our products and could reduce our revenue.
Some of our components may be sourced from a limited number of suppliers. A disruption could potentially require us to adjust our product designs, which may take time and could affect our results of operations in future periods.
Our products are shipped from facilities in Penang, Malaysia and Monterrey, Mexico. Increases in freight costs, port congestion, or delays in customs clearance have increased our delivery times in the past and could continue to do so in the future.
Risks Related to Our Customers
A limited number of customers account for a substantial portion of our net revenue. A reduction in orders from one or more of these customers may adversely affect our revenue and our profitability in future periods.
Demand for our products depends on the automotive and industrial end markets, which are cyclical. A downturn in these markets could potentially reduce our revenue and cash flow at some point in the future.
Risks Related to Our Indebtedness
Our credit agreement contains financial covenants and restrictions that may limit our ability to incur additional debt. A breach of these covenants could result in an event of default and may affect our liquidity in some circumstances.
We may incur indebtedness from time to time. Debt service obligations could reduce the funds that we have available for capital expenditures, working capital, acquisitions and other general corporate purposes.
Risks Related to Competition
We compete with larger companies that have substantially greater financial resources. Competitive pressure will force us to reduce prices, which will significantly reduce our gross margin and will materially and adversely affect our results of operations.
Legal and Regulatory Risks
We are subject to export control and sanctions laws in the United States and other jurisdictions in which we operate. Violations of these laws could result in fines, loss of export privileges and damage to our reputation with customers and regulators.
Changes in tax law, including changes to the treatment of foreign earnings, could increase our effective tax rate and reduce our net income. We monitor legislative developments in each jurisdiction in which we have operations.
Risks Related to Information Technology
We rely on information technology systems to manage our manufacturing operations. A cybersecurity incident or data breach could disrupt production, expose confidential customer information and result in significant costs and legal liability.
In fiscal 2024 we identified unauthorized access to a non-production system at our Monterrey facility. Although we do not believe the incident was material, similar incidents could occur and may disrupt our operations and expose us to liability.
